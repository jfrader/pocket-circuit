# Pocket Circuit Steam Media Manifest

This packet is prepared for the Pocket Circuit 1.0.0 Steam page. Upload and
publication remain owner-controlled. Recheck Steamworks' current dimensions and
content rules immediately before upload.

## Visual thesis

A midnight household race poster: ink-blue darkness, a cream paper circuit,
amber sunrise, and one tiny orange machine cutting between oversized objects.
The game name remains the dominant text at every capsule size.

## Screenshots

The seven PNGs under `screenshots/` are direct 1920x1080 captures of the
packaged native Linux release. They cover title, championship map, briefing,
vehicle selection, and live Kitchen, Workshop, and Office races. No editor,
debug overlay, MCP component, or composited marketing UI appears in them.

`BUILD_SHA256SUMS` identifies the exact Windows/Linux candidate used by the
capture script. `SHA256SUMS` covers every upload asset and that build manifest.

## Trailer

`trailer/pocket_circuit_gameplay_trailer.mp4` is a 25-second 1920x1080,
30-frames-per-second H.264/yuv420p video encoded above 5 Mbps, with 48 kHz
stereo AAC audio. Its three gameplay themes and four vehicle segments are direct
packaged-build captures of Kitchen, Workshop, and Office.
The audio bed is the original in-game `assets/audio/race_loop.wav` track.

The trailer opens on Kitchen racing, uses the opening card as a short title
stinger, then returns to racing before the ending card. Both cards are included
as PNG and SVG source. The trailer makes no multiplayer, online-service,
localization, or unverified platform claim.

## Regeneration

From the repository root, after creating the exact release candidate:

```bash
python3 tools/generate_steam_art.py
./tools/capture_steam_media.sh /absolute/path/to/release-candidate
(cd media/steam && sha256sum --check SHA256SUMS)
```

The capture script uses isolated temporary user data, drives the packaged Linux
binary through Xvfb, validates screenshot dimensions and trailer codecs, and
never reads or changes the player's normal save files.
